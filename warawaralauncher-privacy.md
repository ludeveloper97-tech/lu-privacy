# Wara Wara Launcher Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 27, 2026**

This policy explains how **LuDev** handles information in **Wara Wara Launcher**, including its optional online features. It applies to the Google Play edition, package `com.ludev.warawaralauncher`.

Privacy and support contact: [ludeveloper97@gmail.com](mailto:ludeveloper97@gmail.com).

## 1. Overview

Most launcher settings and personal files are processed on your device. Optional online features, such as NearvyVerse, Community Shop, avatar rendering and purchase verification, send the information described below to the relevant services.

We do not sell personal data or use your contacts, notification information or step activity for advertising. You can use the basic launcher without creating a public community profile.

## 2. Launcher, files and device features

**Installed apps and customization.** The launcher reads installed app names, package identifiers, icons and launch information to display and organize your apps. Layouts, folders, preferences, selected resources and local usage information are stored on your device. We do not upload your installed-app inventory as an advertising profile.

**Files and games.** When you choose images, music, fonts, themes, icons or game folders, the app reads the selected resources and stores the files, references or cached previews needed for those features. Importing a game does not upload its ROM or BIOS file to our servers. Launching a game through an external emulator grants that app the file access needed to open it. Selecting a file for a community upload is a separate action, described below.

**Contacts.** With your permission, Friends List reads contact names, telephone numbers and photos to display and search your contacts. It does not upload your address book to our servers. Starting a call, message or WhatsApp conversation passes the selected number to the app you open. Adding or editing a contact through Android is handled by the device's contacts application and its configured accounts.

**Notification badges and gestures.** If you grant notification access, the launcher reads notification identifiers and the originating app to calculate icon badges. It does not send your notification message content to our servers. Opening Android's notification panel with a gesture displays the system panel; it does not publish those notifications to NearvyVerse.

**Widgets.** The launcher hosts widgets from apps you select and saves their placement, size and provider information. Widget content and any network requests made by a widget are handled by its provider, subject to that app's permissions and privacy policy.

**Sound and input.** Music and sound settings control playback, not microphone recording. The internal keyboard handles text entered into launcher fields; text is sent online only when the feature requires it, such as submitting a post or an online search.

## 3. Optional step counting and Play Coins

If you enable step counting, the app requests physical activity permission and uses the device's step sensor to award Play Coins. Counting may continue in the background or with the screen off through an Android foreground service. This does not use GPS or record your route.

Step totals, the coin balance, daily rewards and local asset unlocks are stored on the device. We do not upload your step history to our community or purchase servers. Daily step and reward records older than 32 days are removed when the local wallet is updated. Disabling counting or revoking permission stops new counting; it does not erase existing records.

Play Coins are virtual rewards for supported Community Shop content. They have no cash value and are separate from the paid Premium unlock. The launcher's manual backup feature does not export the Play Coin wallet.

## 4. Community Shop, NearvyVerse and the plaza

**Community identity.** The online community services create an identifier and recovery key for your account. Registration does not require your real name or email address. Community Shop and NearvyVerse share this identity. If you restore the same identity in a compatible LuDev launcher, it accesses the same server-side profile and activity. Keep your recovery key private: it grants access to that identity.

**Community Shop.** Content you publish, together with its title, description, category, creator alias, avatar portrait and any coin price, is visible to other users. Ratings and download counts may also be displayed. The service stores uploads, ratings, download records, reports and blocks. It receives catalog searches and connection information, such as IP addresses, to serve requests and prevent abuse. Reports and blocks are not published. The association between a downloaded resource and the local app you apply it to stays on your device.

**NearvyVerse.** Your chosen nickname, biography, avatar portrait, posts, drawings, published images, comments and reactions are public. If you choose to share your avatar's appearance, the service also stores its appearance data so compatible launchers can display and animate it in the plaza and restore it with your profile. Other users may see your avatar alongside your public posts and the communities where you participate.

The service also stores follows, favorites, notifications, reports and blocks to operate the community. Reports, blocks and the age range used for safety controls are not public. We do not require a full date of birth. A game title or other information you include in a post becomes part of that public post. Other people may save or redistribute public content.

**Private album and drafts.** Unpublished drawings, images and drafts remain in the device's private library; background community activity does not publish them. They are sent to the community when you choose to publish. They can also be included in a manual launcher backup you export, so protect that backup as described in section 8.

**Community notifications.** If enabled, the app periodically contacts the community service in the background using your community identity to retrieve new activity. It keeps notification preferences and delivery state on the device and can display alerts containing public nicknames or activity information. You can disable alerts in the app or Android settings. The in-app inbox remains available.

**Translation.** Optional translation uses Google ML Kit language identification and on-device translation. Post text is processed on the device for translation rather than sent to Google as translation input. Language models are downloaded for local use. Google may receive technical information about the SDK, device, usage and performance separately from the text being translated.

## 5. Community safety and younger users

Community publishing is for users aged 13 and older. Users aged 13–17 require a parent or guardian's approval for their profile and publications. The approval PIN is checked on the device; the server receives the approval result, not the PIN. These profiles can publish text and drawings under the community's restrictions. The app does not offer direct private messaging.

Do not publish other people's personal information without permission. Use the reporting and blocking features for inappropriate content, or contact us if a child's information needs to be removed.

## 6. Avatar rendering, artwork and external services

Generating avatar portraits and 3D models sends the avatar's appearance parameters to the rendering service at `www.mrrubik.com/3dscommunity/avatar-renderer`. The renderer receives the appearance needed to generate the image or model and technical connection information. This request is separate from publishing your nickname, biography or avatar to NearvyVerse. Downloaded images and models are cached locally.

If you enable online game artwork lookup, game titles and platforms are sent to artwork providers such as TheGamesDB and Libretro. Requests for images also disclose ordinary connection information. Game files themselves are not sent for artwork lookup.

Opening Themezer, a support link or another external website passes control to the selected browser or external app. Those services process information under their own privacy policies. Downloading a theme does not automatically publish your launcher profile to that website.

## 7. Premium purchases and support

Google Play processes payments for the Google Play edition. Our verification service uses a Firebase user identifier, app/package and product identifiers, purchase tokens and purchase status information to validate, restore and protect your Premium entitlement. Google/Firebase authentication, backend and integrity services also process technical identifiers, connection information and security signals. The app can use an anonymous Firebase identity for this purpose; you do not need to create a public NearvyVerse profile to purchase Premium.

We do not receive your full payment-card details. Payment and refund information is also handled by Google under its policies. Google Play handles any optional in-app review you submit.

If you contact support, we use your email address, message and attachments to investigate and respond. You can generate a diagnostic report containing technical device information, app/game/widget counts, general settings, performance measurements and diagnostic entries. The report is saved where you choose and is not sent automatically. Review it before choosing to attach it to an email.

## 8. Backups and local storage

A manual launcher backup can contain your settings, layouts, avatar, selected customization files, game-library metadata, community identity/recovery information and private community library. It is saved to the destination you select. Anyone with access to a backup may be able to read its contents or restore the included community identity. Do not publish or share backup files as if they were ordinary themes.

If you select a cloud storage provider as the destination, that provider handles the exported file under its own policy. Android backup or device-transfer services may also copy eligible app data according to your device configuration. An exported or previously copied file is not erased when you delete the original inside the launcher.

## 9. Providers, security and international processing

Community Shop and NearvyVerse use the shared services hosted at `www.mrrubik.com`, with IONOS hosting infrastructure. Google/Firebase services support purchase verification, authentication, integrity checks and related backend operations. Avatar rendering and game-artwork services process the requests described above. Email providers process support messages you send.

These providers may process technical information and identifiers needed to operate, secure and maintain their services. Information may be processed outside your country under the providers' applicable terms and safeguards. Public community content is intentionally accessible to other users.

Connections to the community, avatar-rendering and purchase-verification services use HTTPS. Access to device features is limited by Android permissions and your file selections. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you remove it through the available app options, clear app data or uninstall, subject to any backups you have kept or restored. Delete exported backups, reports and other copies from their storage locations separately. Uninstalling does not delete server-side community data or your Google Play purchase record.

Community publications and associated account records do not have a general automatic expiry. Deleting a Community Shop publication removes it from the catalog, but does not automatically remove every associated record or copies downloaded by other users. Resource files can remain while other active publications reference them. Contact us to request deletion of remaining Shop account data.

You can delete your NearvyVerse profile through its in-app profile/options menu or through the [community profile deletion page](https://www.mrrubik.com/3dscommunity/delete.php). This removes the profile and associated activity, including posts and their files, comments, reactions, follows and replies attached to your posts. The shared service also removes its protected technical backups following a profile deletion request. The same shared profile will no longer be available in other launchers using that identity. This does not delete Community Shop publications, local coins, your Premium purchase or copies saved by other people.

Purchase verification records, support correspondence and security logs are retained for their operational purpose and applicable accounting, legal or fraud-prevention requirements. Contact us to request deletion of data associated with those services. If a record must be retained, we will explain the applicable reason. A data-deletion request does not itself request a Google Play refund.

## 11. Your choices and rights

You can disable optional features, revoke permissions in Android, edit your public profile, remove content and request deletion. We process information to provide the features you request, with consent where required, and for service security and applicable legal obligations.

To request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, withdraw consent or object to processing where these rights apply, email [ludeveloper97@gmail.com](mailto:ludeveloper97@gmail.com). Mention **Wara Wara Launcher** and the affected service. We may request proportionate information to locate your records and verify ownership. Do not include passwords or your recovery key in an initial email. You may also complain to your local data protection authority where applicable.

We will publish changes to this policy with an updated date.
