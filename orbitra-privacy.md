# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-14

Orbitra is an Android launcher designed to run primarily on your device. This
policy explains what data is used by the app and why.

## Data Orbitra Uses

Orbitra can read the list of installed apps so it can display and launch them
from the home screen. This includes app names, package names, icons, and basic
category information.

If notification badges are enabled, Orbitra uses Android notification listener
access to count active notifications per app. This is used only to display
badges on app icons.

Orbitra stores launcher settings locally, including themes, layout preferences,
motion controls, hidden apps, folders, onboarding state, and premium unlock
state.

## Data Orbitra Does Not Collect

Orbitra does not collect precise location.

Orbitra does not collect contacts, SMS, call logs, photos, files, microphone
recordings, camera content, or notification message text.

Orbitra does not sell personal data.

Orbitra does not require root access.

## Purchases

Orbitra Pro purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. Purchase state is
used to unlock premium features.

## In-App Review

Orbitra may use the native Google Play in-app review prompt after real app use.
Google controls whether the prompt is shown.

## Local Storage

Preferences and launcher configuration are stored locally on the device. If the
app is uninstalled, local Orbitra data may be removed by Android.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact listed on the Play Store page.
